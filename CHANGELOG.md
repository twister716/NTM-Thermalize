# Changelog

## 1.1.0

### Added
- NTM: Space integration (optional): planets have their own temperatures. Bodies closer to the star are hotter,
  thick atmospheres are one level warmer, polar biomes one level colder, and a vacuum is icy (Moho is hot).
- Orbit temperatures: set by the body the station circles, changing with a transfer's progress, one level warmer in
  sunlight and two colder in shadow.
- Room Thermometer: shows the temperature around you in the top left while it is in your inventory, with a needle
  that moves smoothly.
- Room Cooler, Room Heater and Climate Controller: cool, heat or hold a sealed room at neutral. They use power (and
  water or cryogel for cooling), only work in air of at least 0.1 atm, and show their status when looked at.
  Slabs, stairs, glass panes and closed doors seal a room.
- Assembly Machine recipes for the three machines.
- The "HBM Addon: Thermalize" creative tab.

### Changed
- Cladding and air conditioner tooltips now match NTM's own armor modifications.
- Performance improvements for room scanning and suit detection.

## 1.0.0

First release.

- Full-body suits affect Tough As Nails body temperature, with stuffiness in sealed suits.
- Six thermal claddings for heating and cooling.
- Air Conditioner and Thermal Equilibrium Air Conditioner, toggled with G.
- Full set bonus tooltips for heat retention, air conditioning and the temperature limit.
